# Legal review required

The bilingual Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy included in this project are initial templates.
Before production publication, the responsible person or qualified legal counsel must review:

- the controller and contact details;
- the legal bases and retention periods;
- the actual form-delivery workflow;
- the hosting provider's processing terms;
- any later analytics, advertising, embedded media or optional cookies.

No analytics, advertising pixel, embedded YouTube player or cookie-consent system is included in
this iteration.
